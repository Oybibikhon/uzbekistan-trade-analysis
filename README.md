# Uzbekistan Trade Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes Uzbekistan's foreign trade performance and export structure from 2015 to 2025 using official statistics from the National Statistics Committe of the arepublic of Uzbekistan.

The analysis examines export and import dynamics, trade balance, trade openness, export product structure, export partner structure, and export concentration. The project uses the quantitative indicators and the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) to evaluate changes in the concentration of Uzbekistan's exports across products and destination markets.

The goal is to identify major changes and patterns in Uzbekistan's trade structure and present them through reproducible data analysis, processed datasets, and visualizations.

## Objectives

• Analyze Uzbekistan’s export and import dynamics from 2015 to 2025.
• Examine trade balance and trade openness.
• Analyze export structure by product and destination country.
• Measure export concentration using the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI).
• Identify major changes in Uzbekistan’s trade structure and export geography.

## Methodology 

• Data source: National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan (SIAT).
• Period: 2015–2025.
• Tools: Python, pandas, and matplotlib.
• Indicators: export growth, import growth, trade balance, and trade openness.
• Structural analysis: export shares by product category and destination country.
• Concentration analysis: Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI) for export products and destination partners.
• Outputs: processed datasets, charts, and comparative analysis.
