Methodological note:
The project uses official statistics from the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Export and import indicators are used to calculate trade growth, trade balance, trade openness and concentration measures. Product and partner concentration are measured using the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index (HHI).
